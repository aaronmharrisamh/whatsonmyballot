# B.7F review guide

Open [B.7F](../../finishedmockups/Mockup%20B.7F/index.html). This copies B.7E and refines guide navigation, printable outputs, and Ballot Viewer controls.

1. Open My Guide. On a phone, the full-width category bar sits directly below the app header. Solid capsules use white labels and a subtle progress fill. Tapping a category jumps there immediately.
2. Read the admin's recommended and optional choices with their opinions. Your Ballot practice choices stay separate. Desktop retains its two-column category layout.
3. Use **Ballot with Recommendations (PDF)** for a compact two-page view: Front, then Back. Use **Print my Guide** for recommendations and their full opinions in readable, flowing columns.
4. Both output controls appear at the top and bottom of My Guide.
5. Open Ballot Viewer and turn on Snap. Dark Back and Next buttons appear within the active section. White zoom controls remain separate. Moving past a section into empty space brings its ending and navigation back into view.

The sample is fictional. Its data and saves are isolated from earlier mockups. B.8 remains outmoded and supplied no implementation for B.7F.
