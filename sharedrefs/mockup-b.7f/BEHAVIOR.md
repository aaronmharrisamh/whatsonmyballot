# B.7F behavior and data

App version 0.2.5; isolated dataset b7f-fictional-ballot-demo; area catalog b7f-sample-areas. B.7F directly copies B.7E. Ballot, notes, annotations, and area schemas are unchanged. B.8 supplied no code or architecture.

My Guide continues to project saved Admin THIS and OR recommendations with their own opinions. Visitor practice selections, private write-in text, filters, and camera positions cannot change it.

The phone category navigation occupies its own full-width row below the app header. Capsule widths and progress follow measured guide sections. White labels sit on solid capsules with a subtle progress fill. Category jumps are immediate. Wider screens retain side-by-side groups without this navigation row.

Both ends of My Guide offer Ballot with Recommendations (PDF) and Print my Guide. The PDF snapshots saved author records and draws a compact version of the viewer's recommendation cards, with empty ovals and recommendation or OR hints. Front and Back occupy separate pages. It includes every section and marks sections with no recommendations. Columns rebalance within each side to use paper space; the current viewer pan, zoom, and expanded controls are not printed. County and Township context keeps repeated office titles distinct in both outputs. Full opinion text belongs in Print my Guide.

The bundled sample produces two Letter PDF pages with 11.5-point choice names and titles, and 10.5-point selection instructions. Longer edited labels can use slightly smaller type, down to 10.5 points, or Legal paper. The completion notice identifies Legal paper if used. Content that cannot fit two readable pages or uses unsupported PDF-font characters gets an explanation and a Print my Guide alternative; it is not clipped or silently replaced.

Print my Guide creates a separate author-only print surface with two flowing columns. It includes complete recommendation opinions and source links. The sample uses four Letter pages, 12-point opinion text, and 12.5-point choice names. Local browser or printer settings can affect printed results. The local PDF engine and fonts are pinned with hashes and license files; no CDN is required.

Snap mode includes dark Back and Next controls inside the active ballot section. Zoom and All use white controls. After a released drag or wheel idle, nearby sections still take priority. If the view moves below a section into empty space, it returns at the same zoom to show the section ending and Next button, clear of All. Reduced motion makes this return immediate. Snap-off panning and pinching stay free.

The inherited floating zoom and All controls can partly cover underlying ballot detail buttons at some pan positions. Panning reveals those buttons. The new section Back/Next controls reserve enough space to remain clear. Existing two-sheet viewing, nearest-center Snap, recommendation filters, local Admin editing, and dirty-draft guards remain.
