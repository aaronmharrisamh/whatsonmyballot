# B.7H behavior and data

App version 0.2.7; isolated dataset b7h-fictional-ballot-demo; area catalog b7h-sample-areas. B.7H directly copies B.7G. Ballot, notes, annotations, and area schemas are unchanged. B.8 supplied no code or reference.

Every app page has a centered label and its matching dashboard icon. Its row is about one quarter taller than before. Phone My Guide tabs follow this row and retain instant jumps and measured progress; desktop omits those tabs.

My Guide and both outputs use saved Admin recommendations and opinions only. Practice marks, private write-ins, filtering, and camera positions cannot affect them. Recommendation ovals, grouped category capsules, duplicated output buttons, and quiet successful downloads remain.

The recommendation PDF includes full recommendation opinions and retains clear Front and Back labels. Additional continuation pages preserve readable type when the complete text cannot fit on two pages. The compact Print my Guide layout uses 10.5-point opinions, 11.5-point choice names, and reduced spacing while retaining category headings and two columns. The sample prints on four Letter pages, down from five in B.7G. The PDF has six Letter pages: Front occupies four and Back occupies two. Its sequential page-by-page columns keep ordinary race cards together; unusually long sections can flow onto continuation pages rather than clipping. Both outputs retain complete opinions. Browser and printer settings can affect print pagination.

Ballot Viewer retains the two-sheet spread and persistent source-column navigation. Wheel input accumulates through one animation loop with a stable pointer anchor and immediate direction changes. Pixel, line, and page wheel units are normalized. Ctrl-wheel pinch continues to zoom while Snap is on; ordinary Snap wheel input scrolls. Two-touch pinch follows its midpoint, and a remaining finger can continue to pan. Zoom stays bounded by full-spread fit and selected-section width. When a source column starts below half the viewport during Snap, recovery restores its first race and Back control to the top at the existing zoom. Existing bottom recovery and column navigation remain.

Local editing, dirty-draft guards, shared filtering, and unavailable-area behavior remain. The inherited floating tools can cover underlying detail buttons at some pan positions; panning reveals them.
