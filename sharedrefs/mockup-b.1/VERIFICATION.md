# B.1 verification

App version: **0.1.2**. Source: the existing complete 2024 Akron Township 1AF historical sample.

The B.1 checks cover all 28 sections and 148 note targets; selected-section width fitting; page changes; Extents and Section controls; tap selection; keyboard panning and emulated pinch; one-sentence synopses; empty and attributed opinions; 1/2* counts; write-in limits; Admin save, cancel, restore, dirty guards, recovery, and notes import/export.

Timing checks advance the browser clock through the 3-, 30-, and repeating 60-second reminder waits. Animation checks inspect the 1000 ms outline, smooth paper transition, and reduced-motion behavior.

Automated accessibility checks exclude target-size measurements only inside the scaled paper. The readable section and dialog controls are checked separately. This does not claim a full accessibility certification.

Eight browser groups, four additional preview checks, five accessibility states, six responsive layouts, and five notes-storage groups passed in Edge 153. The storage checks include eleven rejected invalid-file cases. The launcher passed its dotted-name, portable-path, occupied-port, and owner-exit checks, and opened B.1 through the real batch entry point.

Reports stay in the developer's ignored `docs/verification/` folder. Physical touch, native phone keyboards, Safari, native browser zoom, and assistive-technology user testing remain unverified.
