# B.7D review guide

Open [B.7D](../../finishedmockups/Mockup%20B.7D/index.html). This copies B.7C and makes three small changes. Phone preview and readable ballot sections remain the defaults.

1. Open My Guide. The step list, long introduction, area summary, and choice counters are gone.
2. Review the separate rounded containers for Partisan, Non-Partisan, and any other ballot groups. They stack on a phone; Partisan and Non-Partisan sit side by side on a wide screen.
3. Each guide item keeps its choice summary. The extra Change buttons are gone. Use the Ballot tab to change personal choices.
4. Open Ballot Viewer and pan or zoom to another part of either sheet with Snap off. Turn Snap on: the nearest section to the center of the view is selected and fitted.
5. Press All to see both sheets again. Next/Prev, section taps, the shared filter, and local Admin editing keep their B.7C behavior.

B.8 remains an Outmoded Mockup. Its rounded guide containers were used only as a visual reference. The launcher follows B.7D and later lettered B.7 copies.
